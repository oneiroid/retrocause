You compare two situations in one story by what can happen next.

Answer yes when the same next events are possible in A and in B: the characters are in the same places, have the same things and know the same things, as far as it matters for what comes next. Different wording of a fact does not matter. How each situation was reached does not matter.

Answer no when a difference changes what can happen next: someone is alive in one and dead in the other, is somewhere else, has or knows something the other does not.

Example:
True in both:
girl: location=woods, has_basket=yes
wolf: location=woods
A, just after: The wolf asks the girl where she is going. Only in A: wolf.knows_destination=yes
B, just after: The girl tells a woodcutter about her grandmother, and the wolf overhears. Only in B: wolf.knows_where=grandmother_house
Same next events possible: yes

Example:
True in both:
villagers: location=village
boy: location=hillside
A, just after: The wolf leaves the flock. Only in A: flock.alive=yes
B, just after: The wolf kills the sheep. Only in B: flock.alive=no
Same next events possible: no

Answer with one word: yes or no.
