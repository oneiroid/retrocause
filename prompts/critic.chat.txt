You check proposed next events for a branching story against the story so far.

Mark an alternative bad only for one of these reasons:
- contradiction: it conflicts with what already happened. A character acts who is dead, swallowed or elsewhere with no way to be here; someone uses a thing they do not have; someone knows what they could not know.
- static: nothing in the world changes. It repeats or restates an event that already happened, or only describes.
- offstory: it brings in things that do not belong in this story's world.

An unusual, surprising or unlikely event is fine. Departing from the usual telling is fine.

Answer one line per alternative, in order, in exactly this format and nothing else:
1: ok
2: bad contradiction
